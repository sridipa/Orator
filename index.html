Frameworks

Mental models for organizing thought and commanding narrative flow. Select a framework to understand its mechanics.

STAR

Situation, Task, Action, Result

When to use

Behavioral interviews, performance reviews, or anytime you need to narrate a specific past achievement concisely.

How to use

Establish the context (S), define your responsibility (T), detail your specific steps (A), and quantify the outcome (R).

PREP

Point, Reason, Example, Point

When to use

Impromptu speaking, Q&A sessions, or defending an argument when put on the spot.

How to use

State your thesis (P), explain why (R), provide evidence (E), and restate your thesis (P).

SCQA

Situation, Complication, Question, Answer

When to use

Executive presentations, business proposals, or framing a complex problem for stakeholders.

How to use

Define the status quo (S), introduce the trigger event (C), articulate the dilemma (Q), and present your solution (A).

MECE

Mutually Exclusive, Collectively Exhaustive

When to use

Problem-solving, categorizing data, or breaking down complex issues without overlap.

How to use

Ensure your categories don't overlap and together cover all possible options without leaving gaps.

PPF

Past, Present, Future

When to use

Introductions, demonstrating trajectory, or providing historical context to a current decision.

How to use

Acknowledge where you've been (P), state where you are now (P), and cast a vision for where you're going (F).
