Mental Models

Proven structures to organize thoughts under pressure.

STAR Model

Situation • Task • Action • Result

Ideal for behavioral interviews, storytelling, and contextualizing past accomplishments clearly.

PREP Model

Point • Reason • Example • Point

Best for impromptu questions, persuasive presentations, and clear structural arguments.

SCQA Model

Situation • Complication • Question • Answer

The gold standard for executive communication, problem-solving, and persuasive pitches.
